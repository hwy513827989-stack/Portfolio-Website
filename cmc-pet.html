Project Direction

A Warm, Illustrated Adoption Website

The course assignment was to design and build a responsive multi-page website. Our three-person team chose pet adoption and developed a warmer, more playful visual direction instead of using the neutral style common to many information-heavy websites.

The visual direction and page structure were developed collaboratively. My individual contribution focused on the illustrated assets and shared front-end components.

Team Process

Wireframes and Early Visual Explorations

The team created desktop and mobile wireframes in Figma to establish content order and page structure before styling. We then tested a darker visual direction and replaced it with a lighter pastel palette because it better matched the illustrated assets and the tone we wanted for the site.

Visual Direction

Colour and Typography

The colour palette and typography were selected as a team. Cocoa was used for most text, cream for large backgrounds, and pink and mint for accents. Barrio and Mynerve were used sparingly for decorative headings, while Montserrat and Poppins handled headings, body copy, and interface text.

Layouts for Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile
