Project Structure

Combining Three Mechanics into One Game

The final course assignment combined mechanics developed in earlier projects. I rebuilt click-based combat, projectile shooting, and platforming as three connected levels inside one game.

A central hub connect the levels. Doors unlock in sequence, while additional cracks appear on the egg after each completed level.

The visual direction was influenced by Yume Nikki, particularly its disconnected spaces, strange characters, abrupt transitions, and symbolic objects.

Foundation

Built on Earlier Work

The final game grew from three earlier assignments. Each one introduced a different mechanic, which I later rebuilt as part of the final three-level game.

Assignment 01
Built cursor interaction, click effects, animated background elements, and text rendering.
Assignment 02
Added random enemy spawning, cursor-based combat, and death effects. These mechanics became the basis of Level 1.
Assignment 03
Added player movement, projectiles, enemy waves, a boss encounter, and narrative text. These systems became the basis of Level 2.
Final Project
Rebuilt the earlier mechanics inside a connected hub and added a third platforming level, sequential progression, and a shared visual direction.

Playtesting

Revision after Playtesting

During informal classroom playtesting, classmates and teaching assistants could not always tell whether attacks had registered or when the next level had become available. I strengthened the sound feedback, added volume controls, and revised the conditions that trigger level transitions.

I also added sequential door locking so each level only becomes available after the previous one is completed.

Takeaways

IF I DID IT AGAIN — 01

Test Progression Earlier

I tested individual mechanics before testing the complete game flow. In a future project, I would test the hub, door order, and level transitions as soon as they are connected.

IF I DID IT AGAIN — 02

Allocate More Time to Interaction Tuning

I spent more time completing visual assets than tuning movement, collision, projectile timing, sound balance, and hit response. In a future version, I would reserve a separate polish phase for game feel.
